      How the numbers are defined

      Net sales are shipped units after discounts. Gross profit is net sales minus cost of goods. Gross margin is gross profit divided by net sales. Return rate is returned units divided by units shipped. The KPIs cover the last 12 months, compared with the 12 months before. All figures are in Indian rupees and generated by a fixed formula for this demo. Production and buyer records are modeled separately from sales: stock is units made minus units shipped, and buyer spend is allocated to match market sales.
